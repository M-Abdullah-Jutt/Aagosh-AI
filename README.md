# Aaghosh – AI-Powered Personalized Parenting Companion

**Aaghosh** is a modern, evidence-informed web application designed to support parents through personalized parenting insights and structured behavioral tracking based on the conceptual loop:

**Assess → Track → Guide → Adapt**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Product Principle & Safety Disclaimer**  
> Aaghosh is **NOT a medical diagnosis system** and is **NOT a replacement** for a licensed psychologist, psychiatrist, doctor, or other qualified healthcare professional.

---

## 🌟 Core Capabilities

Aaghosh is equipped with a rich suite of features to empower parents in real-time and over the long term:

### 1. Child Profiles & Goal Setting
- Create dedicated profiles for each child, establishing baseline observations (strengths, challenges, personality, communication style).
- Set and manage actionable, structured **Parenting Goals**.

### 2. Daily Check-Ins & Behavior Tracking
- Log daily moods and behavioral events with intensity levels and triggers.
- Keep a detailed timeline of events and parental responses to better understand behavioral patterns.

### 3. Ask AI Coach
- Access a contextual, text-based AI parenting assistant.
- The Coach uses your child's specific profile, recent behaviors, and goals to provide personalized, evidence-based advice in the moment.

### 4. Live Parenting Studio
- Experience real-time voice support through **Dr. Sophia**, an interactive AI Voice/Video avatar.
- Have a natural conversation during stressful parenting moments for immediate, calm, and structured guidance.

### 5. Analytics & Insights
- Explore beautiful, interactive dashboards visualizing behavioral trends.
- Automatically track your child's most frequent emotions, common triggers, and the effectiveness of your parenting strategies over time.

### 6. Bilingual Support
- Full support for both **English** and **Urdu** (RTL) across the entire application interface.

### 7. Interactive Onboarding
- Guided "Joyride" tours for the main dashboard and child profiles ensure parents know exactly how to leverage all available tools.
